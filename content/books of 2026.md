# january
### Taiwan Travelogue 
*Yang Shuang Zi*
recommended by a friend after i finished House of Doors. what decadent descriptions of food! i was genuinely so hungry throughout reading this. i also went in blind and didn't know that it wasn't actually non-fiction, it felt incredibly real and incredibly layered, and im glad i was duped. partly for the shock factor at the multiple afterwords, but also partly because i don't think the book would've been as impactful without that nuance. its messaging was simple from the very beginning - the narrator, too, no matter what she thinks, is also a colonizer. it's less trying to make an argument for that and more trying to show what it's like for a character to be in denial / in blindness about that. but! the layering of what is reality by: 1) saying that the "original author" fictionalized the final reconciliation with Chi-Chan as a way of apologizing; and 2) the fact that the entire book IS fiction, and is all fictionalized, even though it reads almost autobiographical... we can tell stories about the past to try and make amends for our mistakes or to imagine a different world-line. it doesn't change the past but it can change the present!
