# january
### the summer hikaru died
*jan 19*
been a while since i’ve seen toxic BL in the same toxic yuri way (vs a heated rivalry way), definitely ate it up
visuals were so precise and clean, great use of foreground/background + goes for plausible deniability a lot. something about the timing for a horror jumpscare ~~ the timing for suspense before a confession / timing to build tension between characters
also i love shows where different characters have their own arcs and you’re not sure when they’ll meet but you’re sure they will (very good example: _Dirk Gently’s Holistic Detective Agency_)
