# january
### stranger things s5: the finale
*jan 6*
how the mighty have fallen. disappointed that i started 2026 off with such a whimper and not a bang, and also because does this even count as a film? but i wanted to fully be done with the series before moving onto greener pastures. 
what stood out most to me was how soft, glowy, dewey the final sequences were, especially versus the flat CGI-hell that was the final boss battle. everyone is so happy and bubbly and optimistic about their futures! to the point of it seeming like they've entered the Tsukuyomi alternate-reality that's the stakes of the Naruto finale - i.e. ultimate 'paradise' in a fake dreamworld. in other words i think that how much traction the Conformity Gate fan theory got signals how much the show failed to stick its landing

### Sorry, Baby
*jan 12 on the plane back from HK*
so, so sincere! the film is committed to realism only to the extent that that allows it to be tender and soft with its lead - so those moments of real love, whether for a cat, or between best friends, or for a child that isn't your own, those are incredibly real; and then everything truly violent happens offscreen and is alluded to verbally. i liked the non-chronological 'book chapter' ordering

### Black Bag
*jan 12 on the plane back from HK*
steven soderbergh! always slick. i'd do a double bill with this and The Man from UNCLE (still so upset that that movie never got its sequel. i'd have picked that over the Kingsman franchise hands down). very cool color grading. i wish i could say more but i watched it on an airplane screen so details are fuzzy
