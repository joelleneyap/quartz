It's the 10th of April 2025. Right now, I am sitting at a high wooden dining table, eating dinner. It's rice, teriyaki chicken with mushrooms and spinach banchan. At some point later tonight I'm going to eat a little chocolate from the stash I brought back from Switzerland.

What is the point of writing to the void, when there is no one but LLMs listening? Unsure. But the way the my life in this country seems to be narrowing in on me makes me feel like I want to leave a trace of how I feel before the inevitable -- whatever the inevitable is. 

I moved to Boston in the Fall of 2024. It was sweltering, and then it was freezing, and then now it's in this bipolar season people here call Spring. September to now, in 2025, has been what I've been calling my Depresso Era. But more accurately it is my Lonely Era, or my Alone Era, I haven't decided yet. I've recently been getting this compulsion to list out all the people I'm friends with, whether in Boston or across the globe, just to remind myself that there are genres of life beyond the one I currently wake up to. It's like the antithesis of Arya Stark's kill list.  

Things that have happened in my life:
* I started my first job. 9/9/24
* Survived my first New England winter
* I saw Everglades National Park with Shi Hao and Bryan
* I fell down the stairs and bruised my ass
* I broke the glass teacup that Ellen gave me
* Shi Hao broke the TODAY mug that I got when I did my internship with them
* My worldview changed during NCT in Italy/Switzerland. I met people I would never have met in the other worldlines of my life, and learned about other perspectives that I would never have heard otherwise. I *paraglided*, and I felt the earth tilt under me while I was as light as a feather
* I had a rough week where I almost thought I'd have to move out of this apartment, till I secured new housemates
* Now: I've been anxious about the Trump administration's tightening on immigration and visas, even though I got my H1B selected, because I want to go back to Singapore this summer. I've been feeling like I can't post online, like I can't shout into the void the way I used to, and I believe that the way this country works is wrong. I want to rail at the injustice of this, but when I didn't there were less consequences, and now when I want to they might come for me. I know I want to go at some point but I want that to be on my own terms.

One thing I know for sure: I am so, so privileged. I am so lucky that I have a place to go if this one doesn't work, that my family is safe in a different country, that if I went home it truly wouldn't make a earth-shattering difference to my life.

What obligations do I have to this country though? But isn't this country the most powerful in the world? What obligations do I have to the world?

I don't know. I don't think I have any answers right now.
