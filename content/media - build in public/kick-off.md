A while ago - what feels like ages ago - I sent out the first of my [[quarterly update emails]], committing to "[[structure & practice|building in public]]". I've definitely failed to keep up with them, but it feels like every time I arrive at a crossroads, the answer my mentors give to me over and over is to write more! To put myself and my thoughts out there! 

So new year, new method. I'll try again! 

> Manifesting my end state: I want to develop a reputation for having solid, insightful opinions about the media industry as a whole, both within my current company and outside it. I want to engage in interesting conversations with people who know far more than I do. I want to narrow down on a subset of the media industry to develop more specialized knowledge in, and eventually I want most of the work I get paid for to be in or around the media industry.

I have to say though, the thought of becoming a LinkedIn influencer makes me recoil a little inside. And well, my real fear is that I don't actually have anything interesting to say, or that I don't have enough of a unique voice. But I think those are valid reasons for fear, but not valid reasons to not try. As they say, closed mouths don't get fed.
